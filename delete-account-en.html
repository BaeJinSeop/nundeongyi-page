Delete your account

How to delete your Snowball account and the data that belongs to it

Read the Korean original ↗ · 日本語 ↗

You can delete your account yourself inside the Snowball app, or ask us to delete it by email.

Delete it in the app (immediate)

Using the delete-account feature in the app removes your account and all of your data right away.

1
Open your profile
Tap the More tab at the bottom of the app, then open your profile.
2
Choose "Delete account"
Tap Delete account at the bottom of the screen.
3
Confirm
Tap Delete in the confirmation dialog and it is done immediately.

⚠️ Deleting your account permanently removes your saving records, goals, ledger entries and all other data. It cannot be undone.

Ask us by email (within 3 business days)

If you cannot get into the app, you can ask us to delete your account by email (English is welcome).

  • Subject: [Account deletion request] Snowball
  • Body: the email address you signed up with, or the email address used for your social login

We delete the account and its data within 3 business days of receiving the request and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Account information (email, nickname, profile)
  • Saving records and the history of purchases you parked
  • Expense and income entries, and recurring expense rules
  • Financial goals and savings account information
  • Assets and their transaction history
  • Your own entries and comments in shared ledgers and trip ledgers
  • Challenge and badge information
  • Your inquiry history
  • Push notification token (FCM)
