Privacy Policy

Snowball (눈덩이, the "Service") treats your personal information with care and complies with the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea and other applicable laws. The Service is operated from the Republic of Korea, and personal information is processed on servers located in the Republic of Korea.

This English version is provided for your convenience. If there is any difference in meaning between this version and the Korean original, the Korean original prevails.

1. Personal information we collect

The following information may be collected when you use the Service.

  • When you sign up: email address, nickname
  • When you use social login: the unique identifier of your social account, and your email address if the provider supplies it
  • For app settings: the language, country, time zone and display currency settings of your device
  • When you use the Service: the expense and income entries, saving records, goal and asset information, parked purchases, shared-ledger and trip-ledger entries and comments, one-line notes shared through the Echo feature, and inquiry messages that you enter yourself
  • Collected automatically: device identifier for push notifications (FCM token), access logs
  • Collected automatically (usage analytics): app usage records (screen views, feature usage events), device information, app instance identifier — Google Firebase Analytics
  • Collected automatically (crash diagnostics): app crash logs and diagnostic information — Google Firebase Crashlytics
  • Collected automatically (advertising): advertising identifier (Android ADID / iOS IDFA, on iOS only if you allow tracking through App Tracking Transparency) — Google AdMob

Note: usage analytics do not include the product names, memos or exact amounts you enter. Amounts are processed only as ranges.

2. Why we collect and use it

  • To provide the Service and manage your account
  • To display the Service in the language and currency that suit you
  • To send personalized notifications (push)
  • To show entries that you chose to share — in a shared ledger, a trip ledger or Echo — to the members of that shared space
  • To improve the Service and analyze anonymized usage statistics
  • To diagnose errors and improve app quality
  • To serve advertising
  • To meet obligations under applicable law

3. How long we keep it

Your information is deleted immediately when you delete your account. Where applicable law requires retention, we keep it for the period required.

  • Records of e-commerce transactions: 5 years (Korean Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce)
  • Access logs: 3 months (Korean Protection of Communications Secrets Act)
  • Usage analytics (Firebase Analytics): up to 14 months from collection

4. Providing information to third parties

As a rule, the Service does not provide your personal information to outside parties. Exceptions apply where you have given consent or where disclosure is required by law. Showing entries that you chose to share in a shared ledger, a trip ledger or Echo to the members of that shared space is part of providing the Service itself.

5. Processors we work with

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS): server infrastructure
  • Google Firebase: push notifications (FCM), usage analytics (Analytics), crash diagnostics (Crashlytics)
  • Google Analytics: visit statistics for this website (uses cookies; non-identifying data such as pages viewed and referral source)
  • Google AdMob: in-app advertising
  • Kakao / Google / Apple: social login

6. Your rights

You may exercise the following rights at any time.

  • Request access to your personal information
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal information
  • Request that processing be suspended
  • Delete your account (in the app: Profile → Delete account)

7. How we keep it safe

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • JWT-based authentication and token management
  • Passwords stored with one-way encryption (BCrypt)
